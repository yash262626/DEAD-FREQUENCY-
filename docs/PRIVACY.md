# DEAD FREQUENCY — Privacy

DEAD FREQUENCY does not collect, store or transmit any personal information.

- No telemetry, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- No accounts and no network connections are made by the game.
- Saved games and settings are written only to the player's own computer, in the standard application-data folder (on Windows: `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\DEAD FREQUENCY\`): `save.json`, `save.json.bak`, `profile.json`, `playtest.json` (a local log of play time, deaths and completion per chapter, used only if the player chooses to send it to the developer as playtest feedback), plus a cache of generated sounds.
- If the game is distributed through Steam, Steam's own privacy policy applies to Steam features (e.g. achievements and cloud saves, if enabled); the game itself sends nothing.

Contact for privacy questions: the publisher contact listed on the store page.
