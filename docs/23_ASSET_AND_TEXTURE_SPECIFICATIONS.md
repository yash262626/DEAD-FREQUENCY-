# DEAD FREQUENCY — Asset and Texture Specifications (vertical slice)

What the vertical slice ships with, measured from the files in the repository (2026-11-14). It is the standard
every rebuilt chapter should meet or exceed. All numbers are counts from the glTF files and images; nothing is estimated.

## 1. Conventions

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Units and axes | Metres; Godot axes (Y up, −Z forward). Blender scripts write Godot axes directly. |
| Mesh format | glTF 2.0 (`.gltf` + `.bin`), one file per asset or per environment block. |
| Materials | PBR metal/roughness. Packed maps: **ORM/ARM** = R ambient occlusion, G roughness, B metal. Normal maps OpenGL convention (+Y). Albedo sRGB; every other map linear. |
| Environment UVs | Box-projected in world metres (`dflib.world_uv`: 1 UV unit = 1 m), so a material's UV scale is its tiling per metre and pieces line up across seams. |
| Texel density | texels per metre = texture size × UV scale (table in §4). Target for a 4K picture: ≥ 1,024 px/m on surfaces the player can stand next to; ≥ 512 px/m elsewhere. |
| Import (Godot 3.5) | VRAM-compressed (S3TC on desktop), mipmaps on, anisotropic filtering 16×. Re-import with `tools/import_assets.sh`. |
| Texture quality setting | *Full*, or *Half* (`scripts/texq.gd`): every mipmapped texture of 1,024 px or more is swapped for its own mip chain without the top level — half the resolution, a quarter of the memory, no re-compression. Slice measurement: 228 MB → 85 MB of texture memory (`tools/test.sh texq`). |
| Licences | Only CC0 sources (Poly Haven, ambientCG) and assets generated by the project's own scripts. Every Poly Haven id and its resolution is listed in `game/assets/POLYHAVEN_ASSETS.txt`; see `docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. |

## 2. The Listener (creature v3)

Source: `tools/blender/listener_v2.py` (procedural sculpt → Cycles bakes → rig → animation → LODs → glTF).
In game: `game/assets/creature2/listener2.gltf`, set up by `game/scripts/listener_model.gd`.

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Size in game | Standing upright: 2.27 m (7 ft 5 in) to the crown of the skull. Its idle is a hunched stalking stance (hips 30 cm lower, upper body folded forward, arms hanging to the floor). Asymmetric: left shoulder about 5 cm higher, left arm 7 % longer. |
| Body LOD0 / LOD1 / LOD2 | 110,176 / 41,866 / 15,424 triangles (skin + claws). Switched by camera distance at 7 m and 16 m (Godot 3 has no automatic LOD). |
| Membrane ("ear") | 112,804 triangles: a closed trumpet bell (about 0.37 m deep, 0.66 m across the flared lip) with 17 shallow pleats, slits, pores, 17 cartilage rods outside, a lumpy rim, veins inside and out, papillae round the throat, nine mucus strands. **One level of detail only — a membrane LOD is still to do.** |
| High-poly source | Voxel remesh at 3.2 mm with procedural displacement, baked onto the game mesh (low-poly voxel remesh at 8.5 mm + shrinkwrap). |
| Skin maps | 4,096² albedo (JPEG), 4,096² tangent-space normal (PNG), 4,096² ORM (JPEG). Colour recipe baked in Blender: pale flesh, pink blotches, a noise-warped red vein network with capillaries, dried blood running down; cavities darkened and reddened from the AO bake (`tools/pack_creature_maps.py`). |
| Membrane maps | 2,048² albedo, 2,048² ORM: dark red with black pores inside the bell (chosen by the surface normal during the bake), pale and veined outside. |
| Claw maps | 1,024² albedo, 1,024² ORM. |
| Skin material (3.5) | Near-neutral albedo tint, normal ×1.5, ORM, clearcoat 0.7 (wet sheen), subsurface scattering 0.45 (depth-aware, `follow_surface`), transmission 0.2/0.05/0.04 (red light through thin tissue). Membrane: transmission 0.26/0.06/0.045, SSS 0.45, clearcoat 0.8, double-sided. |
| Skeleton | 78 bones: root, spine and neck chain, head, two membrane lobes, arms with four-joint fingers and a thumb, legs with toes. Bone-heat weights, plus every vertex bone heat missed (942 on detached skull nodules) given to its nearest bone. Claws are rigid to their fingertip bones. |
| Animations | 7: `idle`, `listen`, `walk`, `run`, `search`, `crawl`, `attack` — hand-keyed in script (no motion capture), blended by the AI state (`scripts/listener.gd`). The stalking stance and the crawl were designed with a forward-kinematics check of this rig so feet, knees and hands meet the floor. |
| Not yet at the reference's level | Hand-sculpted anatomy detail (the sculpt is procedural), facial/membrane flexing at the pore scale, motion-captured or animator-polished motion, foot IK on uneven floors. |

The two surfaces that fill most of the corridor frame were raised to 4K for the 4K pass. **Below the 4K target:** the room's upper walls (410 px/m), the corridor ceiling (614 px/m) and the 1K maps on most props; each can be raised from the same CC0 source (Poly Haven offers 4K/8K, ambientCG 2K–8K). Cost: about 20 MB of repository per 4K surface set, about 4× the video memory of the 2K set (the *Half* texture setting brings it back down).

**Decals and authored textures** (`game/slice/tex/`): leak 256×1024, rust 256×768, grime 512², creature smear 1024×256, puddles 512², wet mask 1024² (sampled in world space at 0.11/m), wall water streaks 512×1024 (`tools/gen_wall_streaks.py`, world space), signage 768–1024 × 384, frequency chart 2048×1024, CRT logs 512×384, dial 1024×256, papers 512×724.

**Lighting data:** baked lightmap (CPU lightmapper, 3 bounces, denoised, HDR, 16 texels per unit, atlas up to 4,096²) for the static scene; three box-projected reflection probes; lightmap capture for moving objects. The fixtures (corridor bulkheads, red emergency lamps, troffers, CRT and dial glow, desk lamp) are baked, direct and indirect; real-time and shadowed: the torch (hot spot and spill), the flickering troffer, the red beacon and the hanging lamp.

## 5. Budgets observed in the slice (software rendering, for proportions only)

1.6–2.3 million vertices per frame (including shadow and per-light passes), 260–300 draw calls, about 250 MB of video memory at *Full* texture quality (`tools/test.sh sliceperf`, 1280×720). GPU timings are unmeasured: run `DeadFrequency.exe --benchmark` (or `--benchmark --4k`) on the target PC; it writes `benchmark.txt` with average fps, 1 % low and worst frame per preset.
