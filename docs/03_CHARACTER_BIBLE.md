# DEAD FREQUENCY — Character Bible

Each profile includes a production brief for the final 3D model, animation and voice casting. In the current build characters are represented by procedural stand-in figures and subtitled lines with a procedural radio-voice texture (see 11_ASSET_PRODUCTION_LIST.md).

## DANIEL ARLEN — protagonist (player character)
**Age** 29 (b. 14 Aug 1997 as Daniel Voss). **Role** Former emergency-communications engineer; now a contract technician for Northline Recovery.

**History.** Found at the foot of the mountain in a guard's truck in October 1998, a card pinned to his blanket. Raised in Halden by Ruth and Tom Arlen, who never knew where he came from. Hummed a tune nobody taught him. Grew up fixing radios; became the best rescue-comms engineer in the district. In 2023, during the Harrow Pass avalanche, his own voice on a dead channel told him to hold the rescue team; he did; four hikers died. He quit, stopped sleeping, and lives with tinnitus he calls "the static".

**Personality.** Practical, precise, quietly funny under pressure ("Somebody typed my name into a database once, and the database got lonely"). Talks to himself to stay steady. Distrusts his own instincts since Harrow Pass — the game's central wound.

**Motivation.** Do the job, get out. Then: get Mira out. Then: understand why the line has always known his name.

**Arc.** From "I listened to the wrong voice" → learning which voices are real (89.1 echoes, tapes, Mira) → choosing not to listen alone. In ending D he literally answers his mother.

**Appearance / model brief.** 1.80 m, lean, tired. Dark hair, a few days' stubble, faint scar through the left eyebrow (avalanche). Grey-green eyes. Clothing: weathered orange Northline work jacket over a charcoal hoodie, cargo trousers with a tool loop, waterproof boots, fingerless work gloves. A coiled HX-7 field receiver on a chest strap; head-torch mount on the shoulder strap. First-person: visible gloved hands, jacket cuffs, the receiver face (rendered in HUD in this build).

**Animation set.** First-person: idle breathing, walk/sprint bob, crouch, lean, flashlight raise, radio raise and dial-turn, interact (pull/push/turn wheel), hide-enter/exit, stumble on hit, winded breathing. Cinematic: look-around, flinch, sit-down-against-wall, mic held to mouth (ending C).

**Voice direction.** Mid baritone, low and even; dry humour; breath audible when frightened. Whispered lines must stay intelligible. Never shouts except in the Ch 5 chase. Reference age 28–35. (538 lines across the cast; Daniel has the most.)

## DR. EVELYN VOSS — director of HELIX-9 (1998); Daniel's mother
**Age** 37 in 1998 (b. 1961). Radio astronomer turned signals physicist; discovered the echo shelf in 1993.

**Personality.** Brilliant, impatient, warm in private and cold in a briefing. Calls the Listener "the correspondent". Believes understanding is worth almost any risk — until her son is the risk.

**Secret / role in the mystery.** Brought her baby to the facility because only the carrier made him sleep; signed Run 19 over Hale's refusal; recorded a tape and a vault message for a grown son she hoped would never come back. Wrote the lullaby's words and never sang them near a radio.

**Arc (through tapes).** Ambition (Run 7) → doubt (Run 18) → understanding (the rule) → apology (the vault) → in ending D, a mother who hears her son and closes the door with him.

**Appearance.** 1.68 m. Dark hair pinned up badly, reading glasses on a cord, a man's wristwatch. Grey wool cardigan over a white lab coat with an ECHO project badge; flat shoes. Echo/"shade" appearance: translucent blue-white figure, slightly out of sync with her voice.

**Voice direction.** Precise mezzo, faster when excited, very soft for the lullaby (sung unaccompanied, slightly off-key, intimate — never performed). Briefing (1996) recordings: crisper, more public.

## MARCUS HALE — chief of security
**Age** 46 in 1998. Ex-military signals security.

**Personality.** Blunt, protective, rule-maker ("three rules — they are not suggestions"). Writes letters to his estranged daughter Kate that he never sends.

**Role.** At 02:49 he sealed 23 people behind the containment bulkhead — Mira among them, looking at him through the glass — believing it would contain the Listener. It didn't. He recorded a last report and died at 02:51. His final order: *if you hear me on the radio after tonight, it isn't me. Do the opposite.*

**The radio "Hale" (103.7 MHz).** The Listener wearing Hale's recorded voice. It is helpful and plausible for six chapters, steers Daniel to fix the uplink and reach the chamber, and urges him to open the bulkhead (Ch 10) — the reveal of the betrayal. In Ch 11, confronted, it answers in Daniel's voice.

**Appearance.** 1.88 m, broad, grey crew cut, heavy moustache. Dark-blue security pullover with shoulder patches, webbing belt, radio holster, worn badge #5119 with the photo rubbed thin by his thumb.

**Voice direction.** Low, gravelly, clipped. The real Hale (tapes) has weariness and guilt; the radio Hale must sound *the same* but slightly too smooth — no breath between sentences.

## MIRA SOLACE — signals technician; the survivor
**Age** 24 in 1998 (b. 9 March 1974 — her birthday is the caretaker's code, 0309), and still 24: for her it has been one night, repeated more than eleven thousand times.

**Personality.** Funny, nervous, loyal to the point of self-destruction. Hums when frightened. Calls the baby "Danny".

**Connection to Daniel.** She carried fourteen-month-old Danny up the lift at 02:44, pinned his name to his blanket, gave him to Ines Duarte at the gate, and went back down for Evelyn — twenty seconds before Hale shut the bulkhead. In 2026 she reaches Daniel on 96.1 from inside the 02:43 loop; she realises who he is when he finishes Evelyn's lullaby and tells her his birthday (Ch 9).

**Motivation.** Get Evelyn and the others out; then, above everything, keep Danny from the Door.

**The two Miras.** The Listener imitates her (knocking door, Ch 4; the bulkhead, Ch 10). Once Mira sings the lullaby on air, the song stops being proof — the player must use 107.9 to see who is really speaking.

**Appearance.** 1.62 m, curly dark hair tied back with a pencil through it, freckles, a too-big HELIX-9 fleece over overalls, headphones round her neck. As a 107.9 "shade": blue-white, faintly flickering, always slightly to Daniel's left.

**Voice direction.** Bright alto, quick, cracks when tired; must be able to sing the lullaby softly and naturally. Radio processing: band-limited, faint carrier whine.

## THE LISTENER — antagonist
**What it is.** Everything that ever went out over the air and never came back, given hunger and a shape (see Story Bible → rules). It can only take words, never make them; it can only wear the lonely.

**Abilities (all implemented).** Imitates voices (repeats lines it has "heard": Daniel's own words in Ch 2, Kessler, Evelyn, Mira, Hale); hunts by sound; tracks radio transmissions; learns habits (hiding spots, radio overuse, where you go); manipulates the environment (power failures, doors, knocking, tapes left playing); psychological illusions (forecasts, the warning); cloaked in containment; blind but hyper-acute hearing at the end.

**Current in-game model (procedural, implemented).** Jointed hips/knees/shoulders/elbows, a chest of horizontal rib slats like a radiator grille, forearms longer than the upper arms, three long fingers, a long neck and a **parabolic dish face with a feed horn held on three struts** — a satellite dish where the face should be. Rim-lit black skin shader so the silhouette reads in darkness; the feed horn glows red when it hunts. Movement: long unhurried strides with knee fold, forward pitch when hunting, a dish that snaps toward sound and then drifts, and "signal-stutter" micro-jumps while stalking or investigating.

**Silhouette / model brief.** 2.6 m. Impossibly thin, long-limbed, slightly stooped. Matte, light-swallowing black with a faint wet sheen. Joints too many: forearms longer than upper arms, three long fingers. Ribbed torso like a radiator grille. No face — a shallow black **dish** where the face should be, which rotates toward sound and glows faintly red inside when it hunts. Moves in uneven bursts; freezes when looked at while stalking.

**Animation set.** Idle "listening" (dish slow scan), patrol walk, investigate (head-tilt, crouch), search (sweeping), stalk (low, freeze-when-watched), hunt run (arms forward), ambush wait (perfectly still), grab/attack, retreat, locker drag-out, "face" turn.

**Sound.** Irregular high clicks (echolocation-like; the 94.6 band picks them up), wet footfalls, a rising screech when it commits to a hunt. Its voice is always someone else's, slightly band-limited.
